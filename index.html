No seriously, I keep learning more and more about you, and every time I do it feels like I'm falling further in love with you. You are my favorite book, my favorite movie, my favorite song. You excite me, scare me, craze me, captivate me. Being with you feels light, but also like anything could happen, and everything will. Even without you you're still here. You're the goofiest silliest girl around, and I truly truly truly hope you keep this whimsy going because it makes me so so happy, and I think the world could use a little more of it.

What's so special about you, monkey, and what I love so much about you, is you are so much more than a silly girl. You care so damn much about everyone and everything around you, even to your own detriment sometimes (okay maybe oftentimes). Seriously, Danae, all corniness aside you've taught me so much about love, and relationships, and change. I'm a completely different person than how you found me, in a good way (I hope). You are my bestfriend, lover, and goofball all in one, and I treasure you so god damn much.

I love every moment we get to spend together, I love every word that comes from your mouth, I love every smile coming from your lips. You are my love. Monkey. I love you. I love you I love you I love you. So freaking much. I tell you this all the time, and you do the same to me, but it's hard to really feel the magnitude of those words. But I'll still tell you, every damn second of the day. I love you Danae Kosta. I love you monkey. I love you bestfriend. I love you girlfriend. I love you so fucking much.
